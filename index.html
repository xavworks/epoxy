IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Terms & Conditions.

01. Cancellation, Rescheduling & Deposit

A deposit is required to secure the scheduled service date. Last-minute cancellations or rescheduling may result in applicable fees being deducted from the deposit. A RM150 fee applies if cancellation or rescheduling occurs after the technician arrives on the scheduled day.

02. Site Preparation & Access

The work area must be clean, cleared of personal belongings, completely dry and free from active leaks or standing water. Plumbing and water-related issues must be repaired before installation.

03. Existing Site Conditions

Customers must disclose leakage, moisture problems, damaged tiles, cracks, plumbing issues and existing coatings. Additional preparation or repairs may incur charges.

04. Epoxy Colour & Flake Selection

Preferred epoxy and flake colours must be confirmed at least 3 days before installation. Late changes may incur additional charges.

05. Additional Work

Any work outside the agreed quotation or scope may incur additional charges.

06. Curing Period & Proper Use

The epoxy area must not be stepped on, used, washed or exposed to water for at least 24 hours after completion, unless otherwise advised by the technician. Heavy items must not be placed on the floor for at least 7 days.

07. Payment

The remaining balance must be paid according to the agreed payment terms, normally upon completion.

08. Warranty

Workmanship is covered by a 1-year warranty, subject to inspection. Damage from misuse, impact, leaks, excessive moisture, premature use, improper cleaning or unauthorised modifications is excluded.

09. Photos & Videos

Photos and videos may be taken for documentation, quality control and portfolio purposes. Identifiable personal information will not be intentionally displayed without permission.

10. Quotation & Scope of Work

Prices are based on the agreed scope and known site conditions. Changes may result in a revised quotation.

11. Customer Approval

Booking confirmation, deposit payment or quotation acceptance indicates acknowledgement of the agreed quotation, scope and terms.

12. Right to Refuse or Postpone Work

Work may be refused or postponed if the site is unsafe, inaccessible or unsuitable for proper installation.
